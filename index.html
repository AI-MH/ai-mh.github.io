Our mission

Bridging the gap between technology and clinical practice.

AIMH (AI for Mental Health) is a research community dedicated to utilizing AI to empower mental health. Our work spans natural language processing and extends into broader AI applications, with a focus on developing innovative solutions for mental health challenges.

Through rigorous research and open collaboration, we aim to bridge the gap between technology and clinical practice, ultimately improving outcomes for individuals worldwide. Join us on Discord and explore our latest datasets and models on Hugging Face.

Our works

Projects, datasets & models

Dataset · Defense mechanisms

PsyDefConv

Psychological Defense Mechanisms in Supportive Conversations: a dialogue corpus with help-seeker utterances labeled for defense level, and DMRS Co-Pilot, a four-stage pipeline that provides evidence-based pre-annotations.

Paper Dataset
Synthetic dialogue · Psychotherapy

SQPsych: Structured Questionnaire-based Psychotherapy

Roleplaying with Structure: Synthetic Therapist-Client Conversation Generation from Questionnaires, with the SQPsychConv corpora and SQPsychLLM models.

Project page
Dataset · Social media

SWMH Dataset

Reddit SuicideWatch and Mental Health Collection: a comprehensive dataset collected from Reddit for mental health research.

View on Hugging Face
Pretrained models

MentalBERT

Publicly available pretrained language models: a collection of encoder-only models pretrained for mental health applications.
